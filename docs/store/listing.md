## 中文

名称：TranslateFlow — 网页与 AI 翻译

简短说明：网页双语阅读、划词与 AI 文本翻译，自选翻译服务，复用本地缓存，并用本地词典和学习记录回顾内容。

详细介绍：

TranslateFlow 帮助你阅读、翻译和理解外语内容。你可以选择在线翻译服务、自己配置的 AI 服务，或设备支持的 Chrome 本地翻译；不同功能会清楚显示各自的准备条件。

主要功能：

- 网页双语阅读：翻译正文，保留原文便于对照，并支持显示、隐藏、取消和重试。
- 划词查询：选择文字查看译文或本地词典结果；需要更深入理解时，由你主动触发 AI 解读。
- AI 文本翻译：在独立小窗输入或粘贴文字，选择目标语言和邮件、办公等场景，复制结果或继续调整指定译文。
- 缓存复用：已缓存的译文可在本机复用；仅恢复缓存时，不会为缺失内容自动调用翻译服务。
- 本地词典与学习：导入兼容且有权使用的词典，管理收藏与已启用的阅读记录。
- YouTube 字幕辅助：在视频提供可用字幕且翻译方式已准备好时，使用双语、仅译文或原文模式。依赖视频字幕、网站和服务可用性。

开始使用：安装后打开设置，选择翻译方式并完成相应准备。使用 AI 时，需要自行配置兼容服务和模型；云端服务可能收费，ChatGPT 等聊天会员不等于 API 权限。免费网页接口可能失效或限流。Chrome 本地翻译取决于浏览器、设备、语言和模型资源；本地词典需要导入，扩展不内置完整词典。

隐私与控制：翻译请求直接发往所选服务；自动免费模式可能按可用性选择其他候选服务。服务可能接收待翻译内容及相关上下文，详情见隐私政策。设置、缓存、词典和学习数据保存在本机；不内置广告或使用分析。你可以关闭自动行为、管理数据和清理记录。公开版不包含实验性的 ChatGPT 订阅本地连接。

完整操作步骤：扩展设置 → 使用文档。
隐私与支持：https://coderlambert.github.io/translateflow-plugin/

## English

Name: TranslateFlow — Web & AI Translation

Short description: Bilingual web reading, selection lookup and AI text translation with your chosen services, local caches and dictionaries.

Detailed description:

TranslateFlow helps you read, translate and understand foreign-language content. Choose online translation, an AI service you configure, or Chrome local translation where your device supports it. Each feature explains what it needs before use.

Features:

- Bilingual web reading: compare translated paragraphs with the original, show or hide results, cancel and retry.
- Selection lookup: view translations or imported dictionary entries, then explicitly request AI assistance when you want more detail.
- AI text translation: type or paste into a separate window, choose a language and a scene such as email or work, copy results or refine a particular translation.
- Local cache reuse: restore existing translations without automatically calling a service to fill cache misses.
- Local dictionaries and learning: import compatible dictionaries you have rights to use and manage saved words and enabled reading records.
- YouTube caption assistance: bilingual, translated-only and original modes when the video provides usable captions and a supported translation method is ready. Availability depends on the video, website and service.

Getting started: open settings, select a method and complete its setup. AI features require a compatible service and model you configure. Cloud API usage may cost money; a chatbot subscription does not automatically include API access. Free website translation services may stop working or impose limits. Chrome local translation depends on your browser, device, languages and model resources. Dictionary lookup requires an import; no complete dictionary is bundled.

Privacy and control: requests go directly to the selected service. Free-online automatic mode may select another candidate according to availability. Services can receive submitted content and relevant context as described in the privacy policy. Settings, caches, dictionaries and learning data stay on your device; the extension includes no advertising or usage analytics. You can disable automatic behavior and manage or remove saved data. The public edition excludes the experimental native ChatGPT subscription connection.

User guide: extension settings → User guide.
Privacy and support: https://coderlambert.github.io/translateflow-plugin/
