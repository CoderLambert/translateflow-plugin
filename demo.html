A calmer way to read on the web

Learning a language does not mean stopping at every unfamiliar word. Read a short passage first, notice the main idea, and return to the details that matter.

A bilingual view can make this easier. Keep the original nearby, compare the meaning, and use a dictionary when a single word deserves a closer look.

Choose your translation service thoughtfully. Save useful results, take breaks, and keep control of the text you share.
